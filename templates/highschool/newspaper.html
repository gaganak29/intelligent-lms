Primary Summarization Algorithm:

TextRank Graph-Based Ranking for Extractive Text Summarization

Foundational Research Reference:

R. Mihalcea and P. Tarau, "TextRank: Bringing Order into Texts," in Proceedings of the 2004 Conference on Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing (EMNLP), 2004, pp. 404–411.

IEEE Conference Reference:

M. S. Joshi, K. S. Babu, and B. S. Rao, "An Enhanced TextRank Algorithm for Automated Text Summarization," in 2020 IEEE 5th International Conference on Computing Communication and Automation (ICCCA), Greater Noida, India, 2020, pp. 782–786, doi: 10.1109/ICCCA49541.2020.9250810.

How it Works (Viva Voce Explanation):
  1. Text Normalization: Input document text is segmented into candidate sentences, stripped of stopwords, and tokenized.
  2. Graph Construction: Sentences form graph vertices $V$. Weighted undirected edges $E$ are created based on cosine similarity of term frequency vectors.
  3. PageRank Power Iteration: Iteratively computes centrality weights until numerical convergence.
  4. Summary Extraction: Highest-scoring sentences are selected in original chronological order to preserve narrative cohesion.
