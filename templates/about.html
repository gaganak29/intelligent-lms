1. Abstract

Traditional Learning Management Systems (LMS) predominantly cater to institutional administration, teacher-student grading hierarchies, and static material hosting rather than autonomous, learner-centric cognitive growth. This project presents IntelliLearn, an intelligent, self-learning, and self-assessment web platform tailored for two pivotal academic stages: High School Students and PUC & Degree (Engineering) Students.

The architecture uniquely integrates:

  • Subject-Segmented Assessments: CET and JEE assessments group unique questions into Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics sections, while DSA is grouped by topic. PUC/Degree scores are retained and reported by assessment and subject.
  • Graph-Based TextRank NLP Summarization: Extractive summarization and infographic keyphrase mapping grounded in genuine IEEE literature (Mihalcea & Tarau, 2004; Joshi et al., IEEE ICCCA 2020).
  • Linguistic Map Method: Dynamic parsing of complex textbook theories into adaptive visual process flowcharts, hierarchical taxonomy maps, or branching mind maps based on grammatical indicators.
  • Educational Data Mining (EDM): Decision Tree and K-Means clustering algorithms that analyze student accuracy, variance, and attempts to classify mastery tiers and deliver personalized remediation.

Used for extractive newspaper summarization & keyword extraction.

WS(Vi) = (1-d) + d * Σ [ (w_ji / Σ w_jk) * WS(Vj) ]

Answer: TextRank is an unsupervised, graph-based extractive summarization algorithm derived from Google's PageRank (Mihalcea & Tarau, EMNLP 2004). Sentences in the news article form graph vertices. Edges between sentences are weighted based on their term overlap / cosine similarity of token frequency vectors. Power iteration calculates the global centrality score of each sentence. Sentences with the highest score are extracted in original chronological order, providing a faithful, non-hallucinated summary.
